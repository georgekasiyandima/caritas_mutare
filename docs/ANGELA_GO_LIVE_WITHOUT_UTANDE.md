# How we can go live without waiting on Utande

A note for Angela and the Caritas Mutare team.

You asked, in effect: **can we just go live?** Yes. We do not need Utande
to start using the new website. We only need Utande later, when we want
the address **www.caritasmutare.org** to show the new site, and when we
want the website to send emails.

Think of it as **two launches**, not one.

---

## Launch 1 — start using the new site now (no Utande)

The new website is already built and already online at a working address
that we control:

**https://caritas-mutare.vercel.app**

That address is the new site. It does not go through Utande. Your current
email (`admin@caritasmutare.org` and the other mailboxes) **does not
change**. Nobody in Mutare has to log into anything for this step.

**What you can do with Launch 1**

- Share that link with staff and look at every page.
- Use it as the “new Caritas website” internally.
- Put it in emails, WhatsApp, and documents while we finish the official
  name.

**What Launch 1 is not**

- It is not yet **www.caritasmutare.org**. If someone types the .org
  address, they will not see the new site until Launch 2.
- The website does not yet send emails (contact / volunteer / donate
  notices). Forms can still be saved for staff to read in the admin
  area once the back office is connected — we are finishing that on
  our side. It does not require Utande.

**What we are finishing on our side before we ask you to treat Launch 1
as “fully working”**

- Connecting the public site to the back office (login, contact form,
  volunteer form, donation pledges).
- That is our hosting work. It does not need the Utande portal.

When that is done, we will tell you: “the Vercel site is ready for the
team to use.” Then Launch 1 is real, not just pages to look at.

---

## Launch 2 — put the new site on www.caritasmutare.org (needs Utande)

This is **one change** at Utande. It does **not** move or break email.

Today, www.caritasmutare.org still points at the old Utande address. Only
the person with the Utande login can change that pointer so it aims at
the new site.

**What we need from you for Launch 2 (only when you are ready)**

1. Confirm in Mutare that there is **no old website** you still need at
   www.caritasmutare.org. (From outside Zimbabwe it did not load a page;
   please double-check locally.)
2. Name the person who can log into **Utande**.
3. Either: give us temporary access to add **one website record**,  
   or: we send that person the exact record to type in.

We will **not** change the settings that make email work. `admin@` and
the other inboxes stay as they are.

After that one change, typing www.caritasmutare.org will show the same
site you already used in Launch 1.

---

## Launch 3 — website emails (also Utande, not urgent)

This is **after** you are happy with the site. It is how a donor or
volunteer gets an automatic “we received this” message, and how staff
get a notice without logging in to look.

That needs a few extra Utande records so the internet trusts emails
coming from the website. If we skip them, those messages often land in
**spam**.

We can go live (Launch 1, then Launch 2) **without** Launch 3. Silent
forms are not ideal forever, but they must not block you from publishing
the pages.

---

## Clear order — what happens when

| Step | What | Who | Utande needed? |
|------|------|-----|----------------|
| 1 | We connect the back office so forms and staff login work on the Vercel link | Us | No |
| 2 | You start using **https://caritas-mutare.vercel.app** | Caritas staff | No |
| 3 | One record at Utande: www shows the new site | Utande login + us | **Yes — one website record only** |
| 4 | Optional: website sends emails (thank-you + staff notice) | Utande + us | Yes — extra records, mail still not moved |

**You can stop after step 2 for as long as you like.** The official
name (step 3) can wait until someone has ten minutes in the Utande
portal.

---

## What we will not do

- We will not wait for mailbox aliases, Google Workspace, or a long
  email project before you can **see and use** the new site.
- We will not touch email settings at Utande when we point www at the
  new site.
- We will not ask you to tell the public “www is the new site” until
  step 3 is done.
- If Utande offers to “fix the website,” please loop us in first. The
  one change that would break email is mixing up **mail** settings with
  **website** settings.

---

## One sentence to remember

**We can go live on the Vercel link without Utande. We only need Utande
when we want the Caritas name on the door, and later if we want the
website to send mail.**

We will write again when step 1 is done and the Vercel site is ready
for the team to use in full.
