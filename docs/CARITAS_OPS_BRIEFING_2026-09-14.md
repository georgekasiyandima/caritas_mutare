## Where we are

Contact, volunteer and donate forms now write into the staff portal. Donate
is a **pledge** — a promise to give — not a card or EcoCash payment.
Automatic email from the website is not on yet. Until I switch that on, the
portal is the inbox: if nobody opens it, a message sits unseen even though
the public site looks alive.

The pages you see are one piece. Sign-in and the forms are a second piece.
The records (who applied, who pledged, staff users) are a third. The second
and third are still on **free plans**, so they **sleep** when the office has
been quiet. That is why a form or Staff sign-in can feel slow, or fail once,
after a quiet stretch. A live diocesan site should not do that.

I will make the upgrades. I am not asking the office to log into hosting
dashboards.

1. **The forms and sign-in engine (Render)** — from Free to Starter, about

Please approve this **before** any parish poster, radio mention or partner

## A short training with me

I recommend **one session of about 60 minutes**, not a course. I can run it
with you on a weekday, on the live site — projector or a shared screen. Two
or three people is enough: the portal owner, one backup, and whoever answers
**admin@**.

What I would cover:

1. Public site (10 min) — slogan, partners, donate = pledge, contact address.
2. Sign in (5 min) — bookmark the login page.
3. Dashboard (10 min) — the three “waiting” numbers.
4. A real example (20 min) — one message, one volunteer, one pledge. We agree
   who replies, and in whose name.
5. Confidentiality (10 min) — beneficiaries, volunteers and donors are not
   for family WhatsApp groups.
6. When something fails (5 min) — tell me the page and the time. No password
   in that message.

After that, the weekday habit teaches more than a second workshop. I would
only run another session if a new person joins, or when we add email alerts
or extra logins.

Please propose a date if this is useful.

## Moving off the first shared login

We are still on the **first account I created so we could open the door**.
That was right for launch. It is not right as a standing arrangement.
Nobody can tell who did what, a person who leaves still has the key, and
the password tends to leak into chat.

The portal can already store a new password. There is **not yet** a simple
Change password screen. I will add:

1. **Change password** for the person who is signed in.
2. **Create another staff user** — named people, not a shared “office”
   account.

Until that is ready, please do not invent a second password on WhatsApp. I
will either walk the first change with Angela on a call, or issue named
accounts once the screen is there.

What I need from you:

- **Names** of people who should have their own login (usually two or three:
  portal owner, backup, perhaps finance for pledges).
- Each person keeps their password in a notebook or a password manager — not
  in the team chat.
- When someone leaves, tell me the same week so I can close the account.

I am not moving staff sign-in onto Gmail or Microsoft. Named accounts in
this portal are the right size for the office.

If a campaign or an emergency brings real traffic, I raise the hosting one
step. That is a settings change, not a rebuild. We will know we need it if
the portal is slow **after** it stays awake — not before.

**What I own:** keeping the site up, keeping sign-in and the records safe,

If I am away, the site still runs. You still check the portal. Only **new**
